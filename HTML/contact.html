Frequently Asked Questions

Can I book a test drive online?

Yes. Select "Book a Test Drive" above, choose your preferred model, date and location through our enquiry form, and our team will confirm your slot.

Can I request a quotation?

Yes. Select "Request a Price" as your enquiry type and our team will get back to you with a detailed quote for your chosen model and variant.

Can I customize my vehicle?

Customization availability depends on the model and configuration. Select "Vehicle Enquiry" and our specialists will walk you through the options.

How soon will I receive a response?

Our team will contact you using your preferred communication method, usually within 24 hours during business days.

Do you accept trade-ins or part-exchange?

Yes. Select "Purchase Assistance" and mention your current vehicle - our team will arrange a valuation as part of your enquiry.

Is financing available?

We work with a network of financing partners for eligible models. Let us know your preferred budget in your message and we'll share the options that fit.

What warranty comes with the vehicle?

Warranty coverage varies by brand and model. Full details are shared during your enquiry, along with any extended warranty options available.

How long does delivery take after booking?

Delivery timelines depend on the model, variant and stock availability, typically ranging from a few days to several weeks for made-to-order configurations.

Do you ship vehicles internationally?

Select "Partnership / Business Enquiry" for international or bulk requests and our team will guide you through logistics and documentation.

Can I charge an electric vehicle at your showroom?

Yes, our Experience Centres offer charging support for EV test drives and viewings - just mention this when booking.

Can I just walk in without booking an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome, but booking ahead through this form guarantees a specialist and your preferred vehicle are ready when you arrive.

Can I cancel or reschedule a test drive?

Absolutely. Reach out through WhatsApp Concierge or email with your original booking details and we'll help you find a new slot.

Is there room to negotiate on price?

Select "Request a Price" and our specialists will discuss configuration, offers and any applicable pricing flexibility with you directly.

Can I view multiple cars in a single visit?

Yes. Mention every model you'd like to see in your message and we'll prepare all of them for your showroom visit.

Do you offer virtual consultations?

Yes, select "General Enquiry" and mention you'd prefer a video call - a specialist will arrange a virtual walkthrough of the vehicle.

Can I extend my warranty after purchase?

Extended warranty plans are available on most models. Ask your specialist about options during the ownership conversation.

Do you help arrange insurance?

Yes, our concierge team can connect you with trusted insurance partners as part of the "Purchase Assistance" enquiry type.

Is there a loyalty or referral program?

AUTOVERSE occasionally runs referral perks for returning customers - ask your specialist what's currently available.

Can businesses purchase vehicles in bulk?

Yes. Select "Partnership / Business Enquiry" for corporate or fleet purchases and a dedicated account specialist will reach out.

How is my personal information used?

Your details are only used to respond to your enquiry and, if opted in, to share AUTOVERSE news - see our Privacy Policy for full details.
