Frequently Asked Questions

Is AUTOVERSE a real car dealership?

No. AUTOVERSE is a student project that showcases cars. It does not sell cars, arrange test drives or give quotations.

What happens when I submit an enquiry?

It is saved in this browser's localStorage with a reference number and appears in My Garage when you are signed in. Nothing is emailed or sent to a server, and nobody is notified.

Can I book a test drive?

You can choose "Book a Test Drive" to try the form, and the request will appear in My Garage. No real test drive is arranged.

Will anyone reply to my enquiry?

No. The site has no backend, so enquiries are not received by anyone. To contact the project team, email hitarth@gmail.com.

Are the prices real?

Prices are indicative figures in Indian rupees, shown for browsing and comparison only. Check with an authorised dealer for current prices.

Do I need an account?

No. You can browse, compare and save cars without one. My Garage needs a sign-in, but accounts are a frontend demo stored only in this browser.

Where is my information stored?

Only in your browser's localStorage on this device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my enquiries and saved data?

Clear this site's data (cookies and site data) in your browser settings. This removes your demo account, wishlist, compare list, enquiries and test-drive requests.
