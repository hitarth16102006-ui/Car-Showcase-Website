Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information AUTOVERSE collects, how it's used, and how it's stored. AUTOVERSE is a student-built demonstration platform; this policy is written to reflect exactly how the site works today.

2. Information We Collect

When you use AUTOVERSE, we collect information you provide directly:

  • Account details - name, email address and phone number when you sign up.
  • Wishlist & Compare selections - the cars you save or choose to compare.
  • Enquiries & test-drive requests - the details you submit through the Contact form, including vehicle of interest, preferred contact method, and message.
  • Theme preference - whether you've chosen light or dark mode.

3. How Your Data Is Stored

AUTOVERSE currently has no backend server or database. All of the information above - your account, wishlist, compare list, enquiries, and preferences - is stored locally in your browser's localStorage. It is never transmitted to, or stored on, any AUTOVERSE server. Clearing your browser data or switching devices will remove it.

4. How We Use Your Information

Information you provide is used only to personalize your experience on this device (showing your saved cars, your name in the navigation bar, and your enquiry history in My Garage) and, in a production version of this platform, would be used to respond to enquiries you submit.

5. Cookies & Tracking

AUTOVERSE does not use third-party analytics, advertising cookies, or cross-site tracking. The only client-side storage used is localStorage, scoped to your browser and this site only.

6. Marketing Communications

The "I would like to receive AUTOVERSE news and updates" checkbox on the Contact page is optional and unchecked by default. Since this demo has no email backend, opting in does not currently result in any messages being sent.

7. Third-Party Links

Our footer links to external platforms (Facebook, Instagram, Snapchat, WhatsApp). These are operated independently and governed by their own privacy policies - AUTOVERSE has no control over how they handle your data.

8. Children's Privacy

AUTOVERSE is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as the platform evolves - for example, if a real backend is introduced in a future version. Continued use of the Platform after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@autoverse.com or through our Contact page.
