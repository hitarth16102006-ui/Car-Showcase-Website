Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

AUTOVERSE is a student project with no backend server or database. This policy explains what the site stores and where.

2. What Is Stored

Everything below is saved only in your browser's localStorage on this device:

  • Demo account - your name, email address, optional phone number and a hashed version of your password, plus who is currently signed in.
  • Remembered email - only if you tick "Remember me" when signing in.
  • Wishlist and Compare - the cars you save or add to Compare.
  • Enquiries and test-drive requests - a summary of each request (car, enquiry type, date and reference number; for test drives, your preferred date, time and city) so it can be shown in My Garage.
  • Theme - whether you chose light or dark mode.

The newsletter form on the home page does not store or send your email address.

3. What Is Sent

Nothing you enter is sent to the AUTOVERSE team or to any server. Nobody is notified when you submit a form.

4. Cookies, Analytics and Third Parties

AUTOVERSE does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. Pages load the Poppins font from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google's servers to download it. External links, such as the GitHub link in the footer, are covered by those sites' own policies.

5. Viewing and Deleting Your Data

When signed in, use Export data in the navigation bar to download your saved data as a JSON file. To delete it, sign out and clear this site's data (cookies and site data) in your browser settings. Using another browser or device starts with no data.

6. Changes

This policy will be updated if the site changes, for example if a real backend is added. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to hitarth@gmail.com.
