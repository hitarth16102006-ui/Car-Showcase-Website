ABOUT AUTOVERSE

A Student Project.
Built for Car Lovers.

AUTOVERSE is a car-showcase website built by a team of students. It lets you browse luxury and performance cars, read their specifications, compare them side by side and save your favourites.

Explore the Collection →
01

Our Goal

Clear Car Information, Simply Presented.

We wanted to build a car showcase that is easy to use and easy to read: real model names, clear specifications and indicative prices, without clutter. For every car we focus on four things — performance, luxury, design and technology.

02

How It Was Built

Built Module by Module.

AUTOVERSE started as a team project. Each member took ownership of a module, and the modules were then integrated into one website built with plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. These are the main parts of the site:

  1. HomeThe landing page, navigation, footer and shared styles used across the site.
  2. CarsThe full collection, with search and a brand filter.
  3. DetailsA page for each car with variants, colours, specifications and features.
  4. CompareA side-by-side table for up to three cars, plus a wishlist for saved cars.
  5. GarageA demo sign-in and My Garage dashboard, with all data kept in your browser.
03

Design Philosophy

Sculpted by Design.

Every curve has a purpose and every surface interacts with light. Many of the cars in the collection are recognisable at a glance — even before the engine starts.

Exterior Design Interior Craft Aerodynamics Premium Materials Signature Lighting Proportion & Stance
Form Follows Speed
04

Engineering & Performance

Engineering Without Compromise.

Powertrains, aerodynamics and chassis engineering decide how a car performs. Each details page lists power, torque, top speed and 0–100 km/h time for every variant, so the numbers are easy to compare.

Power, Delivered
05

Craftsmanship

Details Matter.

Luxury lives in the details: leather, carbon fibre, aluminium and wood. That is why each details page covers interior materials, features and colour options alongside the performance figures.

Detail Over Everything
06

Innovation

Where Luxury Meets Technology.

Digital cockpits, driver assistance and electric powertrains are now a big part of modern luxury cars. The collection includes electric and hybrid models such as the Lucid Air, Tesla Model S Plaid and Ferrari SF90.

Electrified by Design
07

Sustainability

Performance with Purpose.

Performance is changing. Alongside petrol models, the collection includes hybrid and electric cars, and every car's fuel type is shown so they are easy to compare.

08

The Driving Experience

Feel Every Mile.

Numbers tell only part of the story. The real measure of a car is what happens when the road opens ahead — the sound, the response, the connection between driver and machine. That is what we tried to capture on every details page.

The Road Ahead
09

Exclusivity

Made for the Few.

Some cars in the collection are built in very small numbers, such as the Koenigsegg Jesko, Bugatti Chiron and Pagani Huayra. Few of us will ever drive one, but you can still explore their specifications and compare them with more familiar performance cars.
